SYNQ

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026, for SYNQ 3.0 and later

Your music stays on your iPhone

Your songs, artwork, playlists and settings are stored on your device. SYNQ has no accounts and never uploads your library. Backups and exports are files you create and choose where to keep.

Wi-Fi Transfer

Wi-Fi Transfer runs a small web server on your iPhone while you use it, so a computer on the same network can send it files. Those files go directly between the two devices.

Analytics

The App Store version of SYNQ sends usage events to PostHog, an analytics service. These include:

  • features used, such as starting an import or an export, and opening the app
  • how many songs an import added, and the theme or app icon chosen
  • a random ID created by the app, which isn't tied to your name or Apple ID
  • device and app details, such as the iOS version and app version
  • your IP address, as part of any connection to the service

They never include your song titles, file names or music. We use them to see how the app is used and to improve it. We don't sell this data or use it for advertising, and SYNQ doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

App updates

SYNQ checks Expo's update service for fixes when it opens, which shares your IP address with that service.

Earlier versions

Versions before 3.0 also used RevenueCat for purchases and the Facebook SDK. SYNQ 3.0 removes both.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your library and its settings from your iPhone. To ask about analytics data, email us.

Children

SYNQ isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
